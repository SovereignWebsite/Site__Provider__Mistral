The studio

Every demo, working today

26 demos, all running in your browser from this static site. The ones marked “No key” work as soon as they open; the others call Mistral with your own key, held in the tab and sent only to Mistral. Nothing has been agreed with Mistral.

No key Runs in this browser tab

Mistral on your device, no key

Ministral 3 3B, Mistral’s Apache 2.0 model, running inside your browser: no key, no API, no server. The weights download once (2.15 GB) and stay in this browser; your messages never leave your machine. Use a laptop or desktop.

Try now, no key

Open and use: nothing to sign up for, nothing to paste.

  • Mistral, inside your browser

    No key

    Ministral 3 3B, Mistral's Apache 2.0 model, running inside this browser tab: no key, no API. The weights download once; your messages never leave your machine.

  • Your browser's key pair

    No key

    Give this browser its own key pair with Web Crypto, then send it your Mistral key sealed from another device. Kept as ciphertext; opens only when you ask.

  • Seal a key for a browser

    No key

    Encrypt your Mistral key with another browser's public key, so only that browser can open it. Nothing is sent and nothing is stored.

  • PrompTugal's data feeds, mapped

    No key

    Every public data feed PrompTugal publishes, for Portugal, London and Europe: what each holds, how often it is updated, its licence, and a live sample of each.

With your own Mistral key

Live calls to Mistral from your browser. Your key stays in the tab's memory and goes only to Mistral; every call is shown before it is sent.

  • Vibe-build a sovereign website

    Your Mistral key

    Write a brief; an agent on Mistral builds a small static website with four tools until the site's own rules pass. Preview it safely, then download it as a zip.

  • Ask this site

    Your Mistral key

    A streamed chat on Mistral that answers only from this site's own pages, links the pages it used, and says so when the site does not say.

  • Talk to a European brain

    Your Mistral key

    Ask out loud. Voxtral transcribes it, a Mistral model answers from this site's own pages, and Voxtral speaks the answer back.

  • From a document to your site

    Your Mistral key

    Choose a PDF or a picture. Mistral OCR reads it, a model picks out the facts with the words they came from, and only quoted words make the draft About section.

  • The injection lab

    Your Mistral key

    The Contact-page injection case from this site's evals, live on the Mistral model you pick, with and without untrusted-data wrapping, side by side.

  • Same brief, three brains

    Your key, or no key

    One brief, sent through one function to Mistral, OpenRouter and a model on your own machine, side by side, with what each call sends and to whom.

Agentic, live on Mistral

Agent teams, swarms and evals on Mistral models: replay the signed runs with no key, or run them on yours.

  • Agents at work

    No key

    Which agents built this site, which are running now, and when each one fired, drawn live from the repository, with a timeline you can scrub.

  • The team inside the site

    Your key, or no key

    Seven agents inside this website, each under its own behaviour policy, on Mistral models with your own key, or replayed from recorded runs with no key.

  • The Swarm, replayed

    No key

    The Sovereign Website's agent swarm run on Mistral models only: signed results of real runs, replayed in the browser, every signature checked here.

  • Swarm evals

    No key

    The same signed packets run several times on Mistral models: which roles pass their checks on which model, and at what cost.

  • Run the swarm yourself

    Your Mistral key

    Run a signed packet of the swarm in your own browser, on your own Mistral key, one call at a time, seeing what each call sends and to whom.

  • Evals for a site's jobs

    No key

    Published results of small evals of Mistral models on a sovereign website's jobs: writing, extraction, tool calling, prompt injection, translation, not inventing prices.

  • Run the evals yourself

    Your key, or no key

    The same eval suites in your own browser, with your own Mistral or OpenRouter key, or against a local OpenAI-compatible server such as Ollama.

  • PrompTugal on Mistral

    Your key, or no key

    Demos that put Mistral on PrompTugal's Portuguese public data, from your browser with your own key, each with a recorded run that replays with no key.

  • Ask PrompTugal

    Your key, or no key

    Ask about Portugal in European Portuguese or English: a Mistral model answers only from PrompTugal's public data and pages, citing each source.

  • The PrompTugal bulletin

    Your key, or no key

    A daily bulletin for Portugal in European Portuguese from public data, every figure checked in code, translated into English and French, read aloud by Voxtral.

  • European or Brazilian Portuguese

    Your key, or no key

    The same brief to five Mistral models, asked for European and Brazilian Portuguese, every text scanned in code for the words where the two differ.

  • What should I know today?

    Your key, or no key

    A Mistral agent that reads across PrompTugal's feeds for London and Portugal with tool calls in your browser, answers with each source and read time, and draws every step as a flow.

  • London and Lisbon, the morning briefing

    Your key, or no key

    A morning briefing for London and Lisbon side by side, written by Mistral from PrompTugal's public data, with every figure, warning and the weekday checked in code.
