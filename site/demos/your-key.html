Your key, and where it goes

These demos use your own key, never ours. We have none, and there is no server of ours for a key to reach.

In plain words

  • Where it is kept: in a variable in this browser tab. Not in local storage, session storage or a cookie. When you close or reload the tab, or go to another page, it is gone, so enter it on the page you want to use.
  • Where it goes: only to the provider you chose, in the Authorization header of the request, and only when you press Send. A Mistral key goes only to api.mistral.ai or api.eu.mistral.ai; an OpenRouter key only to openrouter.ai. A local server gets no key.
  • What you see of it: after you press "Use this key", the box is emptied and the page shows only its last four characters, so you can tell which key is in use.
  • What protects it: any script on a page can read a key held by that page, so these pages load nothing but their own files: no analytics, no fonts, no scripts from anywhere else.
  • How to be rid of it: press "Forget my keys", or close the tab. To cancel the key itself, delete it in your account at the provider.

Mistral's free mode: what to know first

Mistral's free tier used to be called the Experiment plan; it is now called Free mode, and it is where new accounts start. (docs.mistral.ai, subscriptions: "Free mode is the default state for new accounts", as read on 20 November 2026; the old name is from our October article)

  • It may learn from what you send. In Free mode Mistral says it may use your inputs and outputs to train its models, unless you opt out. (help.mistral.ai, training, as read on 20 November 2026)
  • Switch it off first. The opt-out for the API is a toggle in the Admin panel under Privacy, separate from the one for Vibe. (help.mistral.ai, opting out, as read on 20 November 2026) The opt-out does not apply to Labs and Public Preview models. (same page)
  • The paid terms are different. Mistral's inference page says "Data sent through our API isn't used for model training" (mistral.ai/cloud/inference, as read on 20 November 2026), but its help centre says otherwise for Free mode, as above. Zero data retention is only for pay-as-you-go, not Free mode. (help.mistral.ai, zero data retention, as read on 20 November 2026)
  • Kept for 30 days. API inputs and outputs are kept for thirty rolling days to monitor abuse, unless zero data retention is on. (Mistral privacy policy, as read on 20 November 2026)
  • Low limits. Free mode has the lowest rate limits, meant for evaluation and prototyping; the numbers are shown only in your console. (help.mistral.ai, rate limits, as read on 20 November 2026) If you hit one, the demos say "429: rate limited".
  • One key, the plan of your organisation. There is no separate Free mode key; a key takes the plan of the organisation it belongs to. (docs.mistral.ai, API keys, as read on 20 November 2026)

So: use Free mode for material you would be happy to publish, such as the preset briefs in these demos, with the training toggle off. Never for anything private. Whether your particular use is allowed on Free mode is for Mistral's terms to say, not us: read the terms of service before you build on it.

TODO-cite: whether Free mode still needs no card. Our October article quoted "with no credit card required"; on 20 November 2026 we did not find that phrase on Mistral's pages, and the key quickstart mentions setting up billing.

OpenRouter's free models

OpenRouter lists models whose ids end in :free, priced at zero in its public model list (openrouter.ai/api/v1/models, as read on 20 November 2026). In October we found they allow 20 requests a minute and 50 a day, or 1,000 a day once you have bought at least $10 of credit (our October article, not re-checked on 20 November 2026). Whether a free model's provider keeps or learns from your words depends on the model; see OpenRouter's logging and privacy page.
