Demos, London, 21 November 2026

The model is a choice the owner makes, like the host

A sovereign website's files are yours, and you can move them to any host. These demos show the same for its brain: one standard way of calling a model, and the owner picks what sits behind it, on the device, in Europe, or anywhere, with their own key.

This is the claim of A European brain for a sovereign website by Dinis Cruz: if the model can only ever be one provider, the site is sovereign in its files and rented in its brain. Here you can try it rather than read about it. Everything runs in your browser; there is no server of ours, and nothing is sent anywhere until you press a button that says so.

Three levels

On the device

Ministral 3, in 3B, 8B and 14B sizes under Apache 2.0, released for edge and local use. (mistral.ai/news/mistral-3, as read on 20 November 2026)

In Europe

Mistral's EU endpoint, api.eu.mistral.ai, runs inference in data centres in EU and EFTA countries, at 1.1 times list price. (docs.mistral.ai, regional inference, as read on 20 November 2026)

Any provider

The owner's own key through OpenRouter, for Mistral's models again or anyone else's. Same request, different address.

The demos

  • Same brief, three brainsOne brief to Mistral's EU endpoint, a model on OpenRouter and a model on your own computer, side by side, with time, tokens and cost, through one function.
  • Your key, and where it goesWhere your key is kept (a variable in the tab), where it is sent (only the provider you chose), how to forget it, and what Mistral's free mode does with what you send.
  • Level 1, on your deviceMinistral 3 on a Raspberry Pi 5 16GB: an estimate, not measured yet, the exact commands, and a form to compare your own measurement. Nothing is sent.

What each call sends, and to whom. Every demo shows, before you send, the exact address, headers and body of each request, who receives it and their terms, and keeps a log of every call made from the tab, as the Whois tool in the same family does.

What this does not claim

  • Nothing has been agreed with Mistral AI. The article proposes a partnership; that is all it is.
  • Prices and terms are as Mistral's and OpenRouter's pages showed them on 20 November 2026, with the link beside each.
  • Speed on a Raspberry Pi is an estimate until someone measures it.
