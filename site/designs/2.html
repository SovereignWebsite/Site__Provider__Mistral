The map

Where your words go

When a sovereign website asks a model to write, your brief has to go somewhere. There are three places it can go. This drawing uses only what the providers publish, as read on 20 November 2026.

Sovereign means you can choose, and change, who runs your model. The ledger asks five questions of any AI you depend on.

Pick a path

1 On your device

What is sent
Your brief, to a model on your own machine. After the model is downloaded, nothing needs to leave it.
To whom
No one else. For example Ministral 3 run by Ollama. Ollama serves an OpenAI-style endpoint at http://localhost:11434/v1/chat/completions.⁠2
What is kept
Whatever you keep, on your own disk.
The model
Ministral 3 was announced on 2 December 2025 in 3B, 8B and 14B sizes, for edge and local use, each with image understanding, under Apache 2.0.⁠3
Will it fit
An estimate, not measured. The article estimates that the 3B and 8B, compressed to about 2 to 5GB, should run on a 16GB Raspberry Pi 5, the 8B slowly; on a tablet, the 3B is the realistic one.⁠4 Mistral’s official compressed (Q4_K_M) files are 2.15 GB for the 3B and 5.20 GB for the 8B, plus about 0.85 GB for vision. Those are download sizes, not memory use or speed.⁠5

2 To Mistral, in the EU

What is sent
Your brief and your own API key, to Mistral’s API: for processing in the EU, to its EU endpoint.
To whom
Mistral AI was founded in April 2023, and describes itself as “a European leader” in AI.⁠6
Where
By default, data is hosted in the EEA.⁠7 Hosting is not processing: Mistral does not commit to an inference location on the global endpoint, api.mistral.ai. Processing in the EU needs the EU endpoint, api.eu.mistral.ai, billed at 1.1 times list price, without Agents, Batch or the Files API, and serving only the models hosted there.⁠8 Mistral’s regional endpoints were announced as generally available on 11 August 2026.⁠9 Some features may move data temporarily outside the EU to listed subprocessors; Enterprise customers can switch some of these off.⁠10
What is kept, how long
API inputs and outputs are kept for 30 rolling days to monitor abuse, unless zero data retention is on; Agents API data is kept until the account ends. Zero data retention is only for pay-as-you-go, only for stateless calls, must be requested and may be refused, and does not cover Labs or Public Preview models.⁠11
Training
Mistral’s inference page says “Data sent through our API isn’t used for model training”, but its help centre says that in Free mode it may use API inputs and outputs for training unless you opt out (a toggle in the Admin panel, under Privacy), and that Labs and Public Preview models may be used for training whatever the plan.⁠12

3 To any provider, with your key

What is sent
Your brief and your own key, to OpenRouter, which passes the brief to the provider of the model you pick.
To whom
OpenRouter, then that provider. OpenRouter’s OpenAI-style API is at https://openrouter.ai/api/v1.⁠13
What is kept, how long
Set by OpenRouter’s terms and the provider’s. This site has not read them for every provider, so it does not say.
Why it matters
Leaving is changing an address, not rebuilding the site.

Before you pick

Ministral 3, Mistral Small 4 and Voxtral Small are labelled Apache 2.0. Mistral Large 4 is under a modified MIT licence: its announcement says a company offering it as a model-as-a-service needs a separate commercial licence, while Mistral’s help centre describes the condition as a monthly revenue threshold of 20 million dollars. The pages disagree; read the licence file itself before relying on either.⁠14

The idea of a Mistral default on every sovereign website is a proposal from Dinis Cruz’s article A European brain for a sovereign website. Nothing has been agreed with Mistral.
