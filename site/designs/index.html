Five designs, the same facts

Each design rebuilds the page from the same cited facts; none is a reskin. The home page uses design 5, the hackathon, for the hackathon on Saturday 21 November 2026. Which one stays is an open question.

  • 1 The European brain

    Newsroom feature

    Dinis Cruz’s article told as an editorial long read: the three levels, the licence to watch, what was already run, and the proposal.

  • 2 The map

    Where the data goes

    A drawing of the path from your browser to each choice, with what is sent, to whom and for how long.

  • 3 The console

    Playground first

    Pick a provider and see the same OpenAI-style call, and what it sends and to whom.

  • 4 The ledger

    Sovereignty checklist

    Five questions as a table: Mistral’s three levels as the worked example, and your own row to fill in.

  • 5 The hackathon

    Saturday 21 November 2026 (the home page now)

    The briefing for participants: start here, the three demos, a first call in five minutes, and how to share.
