Notes on the worked example

  • Mistral’s inference page says “Data sent through our API isn’t used for model training”, but its help centre says that in Free mode it may use API inputs and outputs for training unless you opt out (a toggle in the Admin panel, under Privacy), and that Labs and Public Preview models may be used for training whatever the plan. (Source: Mistral AI, Cloud inference and Mistral help centre, Do you use my data to train and Mistral help centre, Opting out of training; as read on 20 November 2026.)
  • Mistral says it “complies with both SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001/27701 frameworks”. (Source: Mistral help centre, SOC 2 and ISO 27001; as read on 20 November 2026.)
  • Ministral 3, Mistral Small 4 and Voxtral Small are labelled Apache 2.0. Mistral Large 4 is under a modified MIT licence: its announcement says a company offering it as a model-as-a-service needs a separate commercial licence, while Mistral’s help centre describes the condition as a monthly revenue threshold of 20 million dollars. The pages disagree; read the licence file itself before relying on either. (Source: Mistral docs, Ministral 3 8B and Mistral AI, Mistral Large 4 and Mistral help centre, Licences of the open models; as read on 20 November 2026.)
  • The idea of a Mistral default for sovereign websites is a proposal from A European brain for a sovereign website. Nothing has been agreed with Mistral.
