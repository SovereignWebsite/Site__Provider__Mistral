Worth knowing before you answer

  • Mistral’s inference page says “Data sent through our API isn’t used for model training”, but its help centre says that in Free mode it may use API inputs and outputs for training unless you opt out (a toggle in the Admin panel, under Privacy), and that Labs and Public Preview models may be used for training whatever the plan.⁠11
  • Mistral says it “complies with both SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001/27701 frameworks”.⁠12
  • The idea of a Mistral default for sovereign websites is a proposal from A European brain for a sovereign website. Nothing has been agreed with Mistral.
