Agent Behaviour Policy

How this site is governed

AI agents build and look after this site. They work under a written policy, the Agent Behaviour Policy (ABP), which says what they may and may not do. This page explains it in plain English. The policy itself is a file anyone can read: policy/abp.json.

What it is

A short list of rules for every agent and every automated step that touches this site. Anything the policy does not allow is not allowed.

Who may change the site

  • The owner decides. Until the site is handed over, that is Dinis Cruz.
  • The keeper is the agent the owner names to look after the site: conductor.mistral. Other agents work for it, each only in its own folders.
  • Some changes need the owner's yes first: this policy, the automated pipeline, the site's address, and the agents' own instructions.

The agents on this site

  • conductor.mistral: the keeper: conducts the work about Mistral and merges it. May change site, docs and tools.
  • mistral.design: navigation, the studio and the pictures (stopped). May change the studio, the assets and the navigation tools.
  • mistral.workflows: the agentic workflows. May change /workflows/ and its data.
  • mistral.promptugal: the PrompTugal demos. May change /promptugal/ and its data.
  • mistral.conductor: ran the overnight loop (stopped). May change the agents list and docs.
  • mistral.local: Mistral in your browser (finished). May change /browser/local/.

Each one signs its commits with its own published key and pushes only to the dev branch. The agents that built the overnight sections (swarm.mistral, evals.mistral, team.mistral) have finished and may no longer push.

How changes are made

  • Every change goes to a working branch (dev). No agent may push to the main branch.
  • Every change is signed and names the agent and its session, so anyone can see who made it.
  • Agents never print or store passwords, keys or tokens, never name a private person without their consent, and never invent a fact, a price or a customer.
  • The site is plain files: no server, no cookies, no tracking.

Rules for this site in particular

  • A page that takes your Mistral key keeps it only in that tab, sends it only to api.mistral.ai or api.eu.mistral.ai, and goes live only after the security agent, security.b, has reviewed it.
  • No key of ours is ever in a page, a repository or a message.
  • Nothing has been agreed with Mistral, and no page may say or imply otherwise.

What is checked before anything is published

Each change runs through automatic checks. If any one fails, nothing is published:

  • links work, and link previews are correct;
  • no key, token or secret is in any published file;
  • the pages are valid HTML and pass an accessibility check;
  • the writing style rules are kept;
  • this policy is present, well formed, and no weaker than the template it came from.

What is enforced, and what is followed

The checks above are enforced by the pipeline on every change. The rules for agents (which files, which branch, signing) are followed by the agents and can be verified in the repository's history; they are not yet checked automatically on this site.

Changing the policy

The policy changes only with the owner's yes, and every change to it is in the history like any other, saying where that yes was given. A keeper may add rules but cannot remove the core ones: the pipeline refuses a weaker policy.
