PrompTugal on Mistral: feeds

PrompTugal's data feeds, mapped

Every public data feed PrompTugal publishes, in one place: what each holds, where, how often it is updated, its licence, and whether a web page can read it. Press "Read a live sample" and your browser reads the feed itself, now. These are the feeds the demos on this site are built on, and the ones a hackathon team could build on today.

What this page reads, and from where

  • The map: /data/promptugal/feeds.json on this site, made in code by map-feeds.mjs from PrompTugal's own catalogue (https://data.promptugal.news/api/v1/index.json), each source's card, and a real GET of each feed.
  • A live sample, only when you press its button: one GET, without cookies, to that feed's address on data.promptugal.news or live.promptugal.news. The content security policy of this page allows no other host. No key, and nothing is sent to Mistral.

Each feed keeps its source's licence, shown with it. IPMA's data may be used without a profit-making purpose only. None of these is a warning service: follow IPMA and ANEPC in Portugal, and the Environment Agency and the Met Office in England. The live camera feeds list cameras and their state only, never pictures.

    Feeds that do not exist yet

    For London and Lisbon side by side, the most useful missing feeds are the London Underground's line status, air quality for both cities, the Met Office's weather warnings and Lisbon's bus service alerts. We have asked the PrompTugal team about them. Until a feed is collected by PrompTugal, with its terms checked, these demos do not use it.
