In your browser: PrompTugal

PrompTugal on Mistral

PrompTugal is a family of Portuguese news and public-data sites: PrompTugal.news and its public data on data.promptugal.news (IPMA weather warnings and forecasts, fire risk, sea, events, cyber security), each file saying where it came from and when it was read. These demos put a European model on that Portuguese public data: Mistral, called from your browser with your own key. Each one has a recorded real run first, which replays with no key.

Given your Mistral key as a QR code? Its link opens a demo with the key after "#"; each demo takes it from there, removes it from the address bar, and holds it in the tab's memory only. Each demo also reads a pasted or dropped picture of the QR code.

  • Ask PrompTugal

    Ask about Portugal in European Portuguese or English. A Mistral model reads PrompTugal's data and pages through four read-only tools, cites each source with the time it was read, and every number, time and date in its answer is checked in code against what it read.

    Your key goes to Mistral only. PrompTugal's files are read by your browser, without your key.

  • The day's bulletin, checked in code

    The day's facts picked in code from IPMA's warnings, sea and fire risk and the CISA and ENISA vulnerability lists; a bulletin in European Portuguese written by Mistral from those facts only; every figure and every place it must name checked in code; translations into English and French with a glossary; Voxtral reading it aloud and transcribing it back.

    Your key goes to Mistral only. The five data files are read by your browser, without your key.

  • European or Brazilian Portuguese

    The same brief to five Mistral models, asked for European Portuguese and, as a control, Brazilian Portuguese, three times each; every text scanned in code for the words where the two differ. Our own 30 runs, with what we found, and the same test live on the model you pick.

    Your key goes to Mistral only.

  • London and Lisbon, the morning briefing

    Today's weather and warnings for London and for Lisbon, side by side: facts picked in code from PrompTugal's data, both columns written by Mistral, every figure, warning and the weekday checked in code.

    Your key goes to Mistral only. The data files are read by your browser, without your key.

  • What should I know today?

    Say where you are and what you are doing: a Mistral agent decides which feeds to read, reads them with tools in your browser, answers with each source and read time, and every step is drawn as a flow.

    Your key goes to Mistral only. PrompTugal's files are read by your browser, without your key.

  • PrompTugal's data feeds, mapped

    Every public feed PrompTugal publishes, for Portugal, London and Europe: what each holds, how often it is updated, its licence, whether a web page can read it, and a live sample of each, read by your browser.

    No key, no model.

More demos are being built on 21 November 2026, during the Mistral Vibe Hackathon in London, and will be listed here when they work.

The same rules on every page here

  • Your key is held in the tab's memory only, sent only to api.mistral.ai or api.eu.mistral.ai, never stored and never sent to this site.
  • Each page names every host it reads PrompTugal data from, and its content security policy allows no others.
  • Model output is labelled as model output. Results from our own runs are published with their date and how many runs: no benchmark claims.
  • PrompTugal's data is not a warning service: follow IPMA and the civil protection authority, ANEPC. IPMA's data may be used without a profit-making purpose only; these demonstrations are not commercial.
