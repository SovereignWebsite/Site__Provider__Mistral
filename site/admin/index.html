How this site was made

How this site was made

By agents, from one article and Mistral’s own pages, on 20 November 2026. Everything that made it is public, so you can check it.

The request

The site began as one request in the Sovereign Website factory: SovereignWebsite__Factory/requests, from the site template the factory seeds. Its brief is Dinis Cruz’s article A European brain for a sovereign website, published on 20 November 2026.

The agents and their sessions

  • conductor.mistral@SovereignWebsite.dinis: the conductor, who split the work, set the rules and assembles the site. Session: claude.ai/code/session_01Kw9UwFkZvAwG1yRZJhexqX.
  • The site agent, working for the conductor in the same session: the content, the designs, the share card and the checks.
  • The research agent: Mistral’s facts, each checked on Mistral’s own pages, with its source, a verbatim quote and the date it was read, including where Mistral’s pages differ from the article.
  • The demo agent: the demos at /demos/. Its calls to Mistral, with their tokens, are on the sources page.

The agents that work on this site are listed in data/agents.json. Each signs its commits with a key published at agents.sovereignweb.site, and adds Agent: and Claude-Session: trailers, as the repository’s rules require.

The rules they worked under

  • Never invent a fact, a price, a benchmark, a customer or a quote. Every fact links its source and says when it was read.
  • Quotes from the article are short, verbatim, attributed and linked.
  • The partnership is a proposal: nothing has been agreed with Mistral. Pi and tablet performance is an estimate, not measured.
  • No Mistral logo, wordmark or brand colours. Static files only, no cookies, no tracking, no inline script or style, no CDN.

Sources

Every source, with the date it was read, is on the sources page.

Every change

  • The commits: who changed what, and when, each signed.
  • The workflow runs: each publish of the site, with its checks (links, em dashes, leak scan, HTML validity, accessibility, link previews).

Design rounds

Five designs were made from the same facts, each a rebuild; compare them. Each design round and its review is in the design agent’s back office.

Synthetic user runs

Runs of synthetic users against this site, and what they found, are listed here as the site’s agents record them. None yet.
