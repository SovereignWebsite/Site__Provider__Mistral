In your browser

Mistral, from a web page

Demos that call Mistral straight from this static page, with your own key, held in the tab's memory only, or sealed to this browser if you choose. Each one shows every call before it is sent: what it sends, and to whom. Made for the Mistral hackathon in London on 21 November 2026; this site is independent and not affiliated with Mistral AI.

No key Runs in this browser tab

Mistral on your device, no key

Ministral 3 3B, Mistral’s Apache 2.0 model, running inside your browser: no key, no API, no server. The weights download once (2.15 GB) and stay in this browser; your messages never leave your machine. Use a laptop or desktop.

Run Mistral in this tab

  • Vibe-build a sovereign website

    Write a brief; an agent on Codestral writes a small static site into a folder in your tab, with four tools, until the site's own rules pass. Preview it in a frame that cannot run script, then download it as a zip.

    Your key goes to Mistral only. The site you build stays in your tab until you download it.

  • Mistral, inside your browser

    Ministral 3 3B, Mistral's Apache 2.0 model, running inside the tab on your own machine: no key, no API. A 2.15 GB download once, from Mistral's Hugging Face repository; WebGPU if your browser has it, slowly on the processor if not.

    Only the weights download comes in. Your messages never leave your machine. Laptops, not phones.

  • Ask this site

    A streamed chat on Mistral Small that reads this site's own pages, links the ones it used, and says "This site does not say." when it does not.

    It reads this site only; the model's tools write, send and fetch nothing elsewhere.

  • Talk to a European brain

    Ask this site a question out loud. Voxtral transcribes it, a Mistral model answers from this site's own pages, and Voxtral speaks the answer back.

    Microphone only after you press Record. Audio goes to Mistral only.

  • From a document to your site

    Turn a flyer into a draft About section: Mistral OCR reads it, a model picks out the facts with the words they came from, and only quoted words make the draft. With a fictional sample to try.

    Your file goes to Mistral's OCR endpoint only, after you press Send.

  • The injection lab

    The Contact-page injection case from this site's evals, run live on the model you pick, with and without untrusted-data wrapping, side by side, next to the published results.

    The model's tools write, send and fetch nothing.

  • Your browser's key pair

    Give this browser its own key pair, then send it your Mistral key sealed from your laptop by QR code. It is kept here as ciphertext, survives reloads, and the demos use it only when you press "Use the sealed key stored in this browser".

    No request of any kind. Any page of this site can ask this browser to use the sealed key: use a key with a spending limit.

  • PrompTugal on Mistral

    Mistral on Portuguese public data: ask PrompTugal in European Portuguese or English, and a daily bulletin written from its published data, translated and read aloud, with every figure checked in code.

    Your key goes to Mistral only. PrompTugal's files are read by your browser, without your key.
