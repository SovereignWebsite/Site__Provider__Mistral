In your browser: voice

Talk to a European brain

Ask this site a question out loud. Mistral's Voxtral turns your words into text, a Mistral chat model answers using only this site's own pages, and Voxtral reads the answer back to you. Everything runs from this page, in your browser, with your own Mistral key. Each call is shown before you send it.

1. Your Mistral key

Your key stays in this tab's memory. It is sent only to the Mistral endpoint you choose below, in the Authorization header, and only when you press a button that sends something. It is never stored, never shown, never sent to this site, and gone when you close or reload the tab. Use a key with a spending limit.

If your browser offers to save this key as a password, choose Never.

No key set.

On Mistral's free plan, what you send through the API may be used to train Mistral's models unless you switch that off in your Mistral admin panel (how to opt out, as read on 21 November 2026). Say nothing confidential.

2. Ask out loud, or type

Your microphone, plainly. Nothing is recorded until you press Record; then your browser asks you for the microphone. Recording stops when you press Stop, or after 30 seconds, and the microphone is released at once. The recording stays in this tab: you can play it back, and it is sent only to Mistral, only when you press "Send the recording". Nothing is kept after you close the tab.

Not recording. The microphone is off.

3. The answer, from this site's pages

The model gets two tools that read this site and nothing else: search_site and read_page. They run here, in your browser, over a static index of this site's pages, made when the site was built. The page text goes back to the model marked as data, not instructions.

    How it works, and what we checked

    • Recording: the browser's own MediaRecorder, usually WebM with Opus (Chrome, Firefox) or MP4 (Safari). No library.
    • Transcription: POST /v1/audio/transcriptions, a multipart form with model and file (file transcription, as read on 21 November 2026). We sent a WebM/Opus and an MP4/AAC clip through it on 21 November 2026; both came back as text.
    • Answer: POST /v1/chat/completions with two tools; at most four model turns, the last one without tools.
    • Speech: POST /v1/audio/speech with model, input, voice_id and response_format; the audio comes back base64-encoded in audio_data (speech generation, as read on 21 November 2026).
    • Can a web page call them? On 21 November 2026 both api.mistral.ai and api.eu.mistral.ai answered the browser's preflight for all three paths with Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *, allowing the Authorization and Content-Type headers, and a page in Chromium read the answers. We could test real audio only on the global endpoint.
