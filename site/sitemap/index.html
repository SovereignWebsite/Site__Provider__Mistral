  • The studioEvery demo that works today, with what each needs: no key, or your own Mistral key.

  • DemosWorking demonstrations of a site whose AI model is the owner’s choice, like the host.
  • Same brief, three brainsOne brief sent to Mistral, OpenRouter and a model on your own machine, side by side.

  • In your browserDemos that call Mistral straight from this static page, with your own key.
  • Vibe-build a sovereign websiteWrite a brief; an agent on Mistral builds a small static site in your tab, to download.
  • Ask this siteA chat on Mistral that answers only from this site’s own pages, and links them.
  • Talk to a European brainAsk out loud: Voxtral transcribes, a Mistral model answers from this site, Voxtral speaks.
  • From a document to your siteMistral OCR reads a PDF or a picture; only quoted words make the draft About section.
  • The injection labThe Contact-page injection case from the evals, live on the model you pick.
  • Your browser’s key pairGive this browser its own key pair, then send it your Mistral key sealed from another device.
  • Seal a key for a browserEncrypt your Mistral key with another browser’s public key: nothing is sent, nothing is stored.

Agentic, live on Mistral

  • The team inside the siteSeven agents, each under its own policy, live in your browser on a Mistral model you choose.
  • The Swarm, replayedThe Sovereign Website’s agent swarm on Mistral models: signed results of real runs, replayed.
  • Swarm evalsThe same signed packets run several times: which roles pass their checks on which model.
  • Run the swarm yourselfRun a signed packet in your browser on your own Mistral key, one call at a time.

  • Run the evals yourselfThe same suites in your browser, with your own key or against a local model.

  • Ask PrompTugalAsk about Portugal in European Portuguese or English, answered only from PrompTugal’s public data.
  • The PrompTugal bulletinA daily bulletin for Portugal from public data, every figure checked in code, translated and read aloud by Voxtral.
  • European or Brazilian PortugueseThe same brief to five Mistral models, asked for European and Brazilian Portuguese, scanned in code for where they differ.
  • What should I know today?A Mistral agent that reads across PrompTugal’s feeds for London and Portugal with tool calls, and draws every step.
  • London and Lisbon, the morning briefingA morning briefing for London and Lisbon side by side, written by Mistral, every figure checked in code.
  • PrompTugal’s data feeds, mappedEvery public data feed PrompTugal publishes: what it holds, how often, its licence, and a live sample.
