The team inside the site

A website with a team inside, running on Mistral

Seven agents live in this site: a conductor, a designer, a writer, a developer, a reviewer, a translator and a keeper, the roles from Dinis Cruz's article A website with a team inside (as read on 20 November 2026). Give them a brief. The conductor plans it and hands the work out; each specialist reads this site's own pages and proposes changes, which you see as diffs. Nothing is ever written or published.

Each agent runs under a written behaviour policy, on a Mistral model you choose, called from your browser with your own key. Or replay a recorded run with no key at all.

2. Your Mistral key

Your key stays in this tab's memory. It is sent only to the Mistral endpoint you choose below, in the Authorization header of each model call, and is gone when you close the tab. It is never stored, logged or sent to this site. Use a key with a spending limit.

If your browser offers to save this key as a password, choose Never.

No key set.


The recorded runs used the global endpoint, api.mistral.ai. On Mistral's free plan, what you send through the API may be used for training unless you switch that off in your Mistral admin panel (Privacy); send nothing confidential. Mistral keeps API inputs and outputs for 30 days for abuse monitoring. Sources: regional inference, as read by conductor.mistral@ on 20 November 2026.

Send this key to my phone

This makes a QR code, in this browser, of a link to this page with your key after a #: /team/#k=.... The part after # is never sent to any server. When your phone opens the link, this page reads the key once into memory and removes it from the address bar.

The risk, plainly: anyone who sees or photographs this QR code has your key, and can spend on your account until you revoke it. The full link, key included, is also recorded outside this page: in your phone browser's history (which may sync to your other devices) and in your scanner app's own history, which some apps upload. Show it only to your own phone, use a key with a spending limit, delete that history entry afterwards, and revoke the key after the event. The QR code hides itself after 60 seconds or when you leave this tab; the key and the link are never shown as text.
