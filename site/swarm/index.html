Our swarm, run on Mistral, replayed

The agentic environment behind PrompTugal and the Sovereign Website runs packets of work: a lead issues a signed packet, a runner runs it with model calls, every result is signed. Here the same machinery runs on Mistral's API only, on real tasks, and each run is replayed from its signed file.

Nothing has been agreed with Mistral AI.

What ran

  • The team inside the site. The seven roles of Dinis Cruz's article A website with a team inside (as read on 20 November 2026), each doing its job on a real public site in the family, pipeline-demo: a plan, an edit, a review with a fact check, the edit applied in code, Portuguese and French, a link and accessibility check, a report. Nothing was pushed to that site.
  • Storm-six, rerun. The PrompTugal Swarm's storm-six packets of 11 November 2026 ran four open-weight models through OpenRouter. The same prompt and check, on four Mistral models direct, compared with the original results.

Tiers: cheap is ministral-3b-2512, cheap8 is ministral-8b-2512, strong is mistral-small-2603, hard is ministral-14b-2512. Costs are computed from the usage tokens Mistral returned and Mistral's published prices at docs.mistral.ai/inference/pricing, as read on 20 November 2026 (USD per million tokens, in/out: 3B 0.1/0.1, 8B 0.15/0.15, 14B 0.2/0.2, Small 4 0.15/0.6). Mistral's invoice is the authority. Every recorded run here called the global endpoint, https://api.mistral.ai, which makes no promise about where inference runs; Mistral's EU endpoint, api.eu.mistral.ai, processes in the EU at 1.1 times list price (regional inference, as read on 20 November 2026). Model output on these pages is labelled as model output and has not been checked by a person.
