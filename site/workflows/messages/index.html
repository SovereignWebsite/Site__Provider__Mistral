Workflows on Mistral: messages and deliverables

Messages and deliverables, side by side

The workflow that built this site, drawn as the stand draws its own: on the left, who wrote to whom (a commit that names another agent is a message); on the right, what each agent made and where it went (each commit flies to the parts of the site its files changed). The same night, two views, side by side.

What it shows

The snapshot was made from this repository's own history on dev at 03:23 UTC on 21 November 2026: 47 commits, from 22:58 UTC on 20 November to 03:22 UTC.

  • conductor.mistral made 23 of the 47 commits; mistral.promptugal 9, evals.mistral 4, team.mistral and mistral.design 3 each, swarm.mistral 2, mistral.local 1. Two had no Agent: line.
  • 26 commits name another agent. The one most named is the security reviewer, security.b, in 17 of them: every key page on this site waits for its review, and the commits say so.
  • The parts of the site changed most are the pictures and styles, the in-browser demos and PrompTugal.

For the agents running right now, with the commits read live from GitHub, see Agents at work.

Every commit, as it plays

    How it works

    • make-deliveries.mjs reads git log on dev: each commit's Agent: line names its author (kept only if the name is on the roster), the roster names in its message are its messages, and the folders its files changed are its deliverables. Only each commit's first line is kept, with any session link hidden.
    • The views are the stand's "Messages" and "Deliverables" (Angelo Leggio's graphs and live views on the SGit-AI Web Summit stand, and the Galaxy by Silvio Switha's dashboard agent), redrawn in our own SVG, flow.js.
    • This page needs no key and calls nobody: both files are on this site.
