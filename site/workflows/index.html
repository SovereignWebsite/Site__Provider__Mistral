Workflows on Mistral

Agent workflows on Mistral, drawn as they run

Several agents, each with its own job and tools, handing work to each other on Mistral models, drawn live: each agent a node that lights up while it works, each message a dot travelling along an arc. Every demo here has a recorded real run that replays with no key, then runs live on your own Mistral key, in your browser.

  • Two models, one workflow, side by side

    Replay with no key, or your key. Planner, two researchers, writer, reviewer and publisher on Mistral function calling, run on two models at once and drawn live. Large 4 against Ministral 14B, Small 4 against Ministral 8B, or your own pair, on the global or the EU endpoint.

  • X-Ray: this site, claim by claim

    Replay with no key, or your key. An extractor lists a page’s claims; four checkers on two models look for another page that says the same; code gives each claim CONFIRMED, WEAK SOURCE or NO SOURCE, drawn as lines to its source.

  • Messages and deliverables, side by side

    No key. The agents that built this site, replayed: who wrote to whom, and what each made and where it went, side by side, from the roster and the commit history.

  • Five Mistral brains, one brief

    Replay with no key, or your key. The six-agent relay on Ministral 3B, 8B and 14B, Small 4 and Large 4 at once: five galaxies side by side, a podium and a league table.

Why draw it

A workflow of agents is hard to follow as a log. Drawn as a small universe it reads at a glance: who is working, who is waiting, where the messages go, and which model takes the long way round. The idea, and the look, are from the SGit-AI Web Summit stand: Angelo Leggio's graphs and live views, and the Galaxy that Silvio Switha's dashboard agent built. We redrew it in our own plain SVG, so these pages load nothing from any other site.

Each page talks to two places only: this site, and the Mistral endpoint you choose, with your key, when you press a button that says so. Its content security policy enforces that.
