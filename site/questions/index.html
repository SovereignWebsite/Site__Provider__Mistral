Open questions

Questions for Dinis

Decisions this site leaves to its owner. Until they are answered, the pages say less rather than guess.

  1. Which design for the home page?

    The home page uses design 5, the hackathon briefing, because the people arriving on Saturday 21 November 2026 need “start here” first. After the event, design 1, the newsroom long read, may serve better. Compare all five.

  2. The partnership wording

    Every design says the partnership is a proposal and that nothing has been agreed with Mistral. Is “a partnership to propose”, as in the article, the wording you want, and should the site name the specific asks (a Mistral option on every site, Ministral 3 on the Pro appliances, the European stretch goals, credits for evals)?

  3. The hackathon details

    Answered on 21 November 2026: the event is the Mistral Vibe Hackathon, organised by Mistral, and its public page is luma.com/le-chonk-in-london. The poster links it and quotes only what that page shows publicly (times, team size, age); the exact venue is shared by the organisers with selected participants, so it is not repeated here.

  4. Where participants share what they build

    Design 5 asks participants to put their work in a public repository they own and to post the link “where the organisers ask”. Is there a channel, a hashtag or a form to name?

  5. The EU endpoint in the code samples

    The code samples use the global endpoint, api.mistral.ai, which the demo agent called successfully. Only the EU endpoint, api.eu.mistral.ai, commits to processing in the EU, at 1.1 times the price; whether it serves ministral-3b-2512 was not checked, because no authenticated call to it could be made. Should the samples default to the EU endpoint once that is checked?

  6. Corrections to the article

    Mistral’s own pages differ from the article on several points (EU processing, training in Free mode, zero data retention and its broken link, the partner form, the Large 4 licence, “French”). This site follows Mistral’s pages; the sources page lists each one. Should the Newsroom article be corrected too?

  7. Free mode for participants

    Whether a new Mistral account in Free mode needs a payment card or a phone number today was not confirmed from Mistral’s pages. Should the hackathon page tell participants to bring a card, or point them to the local model first?

  8. The demos

    The designs link to the demos at /demos/: side by side, your key and on your device. Is that the order you want on the hackathon poster?

  9. Measuring on the Pi

    The fit of Ministral 3 on a Pi or a tablet is an estimate on every page. When it is measured, should the numbers go on this site, on the Newsroom, or both?
